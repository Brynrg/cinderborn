# Cinderborn — A Borrowed Name

The owner rejected squad tactics and requested the feel of an MMORPG, clarified
as solo exploration, real-time combat, quests and character progression first.

The player is Ash. They directly inhabit the world instead of commanding a board.
The default game is a continuous top-down region with no turns or hex movement.

## The first region

Emberrest Camp connects to Mistwood Road, Hollowwood, the Ashen Causeway,
Northwatch Cells, the Broken Quarry and the Conclave Approach. Roads, bridges,
forest obstacles and ruins form a navigable world. The world map supports travel
planning; discovered hearths provide out-of-combat fast travel and recovery.

- Rook introduces the stolen identity and offers repeatable road bounties.
- Iona teaches stronger healing after a silverleaf gathering quest.
- Sable sells better steel and healing supplies.
- Vesper recognizes the Cinder beneath the stolen crest. Her seal binds Ash to
  save Mara and Brann; declining leads to the Seal Warden and the northern shrine.
- The prisoners return to Emberrest when freed and can be visited there.
- The Arbiter of the Crossing guards the Conclave road. Defeating it and reaching
  the road completes the first chapter, rewards Cindersteel, and leaves the
  region open for unfinished quests and exploration.
- Hollowfang guards an optional cache and a health-boosting mantle.

The five houses, champions, the Attainted Roll and Ash's forbidden identity
remain in the journal. Existing tactical/Conclave modes and saves stay available
through separate entries, preserving the earlier work and story material.

## Player loop

Explore → accept a quest → fight/gather/discover → gain XP and crowns → choose
talents/equip loot → take on harder encounters → resolve a story choice.

Steel is a continuous basic attack. Firebrand is ranged damage, Mend is healing,
and Veilstep is a short invulnerable dash. Focus regenerates; cooldowns prevent
spamming. Enemy windups draw red danger areas before impact. The Arbiter becomes
faster and hits harder below half health. Forbidden fire raises suspicion and
extends judges' detection range at high heat.

XP unlocks levels and talent points. Steel, flame and resolve talents change
actual damage/health. Equipment affects sword damage and maximum health. Normal
road enemies respawn away from the player; named encounters remain defeated.
Resting replenishes three healing draughts. Defeat costs a small crown fraction
and returns Ash to the last hearth without deleting story or equipment progress.

## Controls and presentation

WASD/arrows or click ground to move; click enemies to engage. Space/1 strikes,
2 Firebrand, 3 Mend, 4 Veilstep, Q draught, E interact, Tab target. C/I character,
J journal, M world map, Escape pause, F fullscreen. Dialogues and menus pause
this solo world. Sound is optional and starts off.

The viewport belongs to the world: status upper-left, current target above,
quest tracker upper-right, abilities below. The canvas has original procedural
fantasy scenery and character figures. No third-party artwork or runtime packages.

## Scope

A complete solo opening chapter with continued exploration. No accounts, other
players, networking, raids, auction house, or claim of MMO-scale content. This
release establishes the requested gameplay direction. Broader regions and a
longer campaign should follow owner playtesting of movement/combat/questing.

New saves use `cinderborn-rpg-v1`; old namespaces are neither erased nor converted.
The static deployment remains the canonical GitHub → portal ingest pipeline.
